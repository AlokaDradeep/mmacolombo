Privacy enquiry
01

Introduction

MMA Colombo (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website, contact us or interact with our services. By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described here.

02

Information we collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide: Your name, email address, telephone number and other details you choose to share when contacting us.
  • Website usage information: Pages visited, device and browser information, approximate location, IP address and similar technical data.
  • Cookies: Small files and related technologies that support website functionality, analytics and relevant content.
03

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide class and membership information, operate and improve our services, maintain website security, understand site usage and deliver relevant content.

04

Third-party services and advertising

Our website may use third-party services such as Google Analytics or Google advertising products. These providers may use cookies or similar identifiers under their own privacy policies.

Learn more through the Google Ads Technology policy and Google Privacy Policy.

05

Data retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect information, although no online service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. You may also manage cookies through your browser and advertising preferences through the relevant provider.
