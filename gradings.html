Our advanced club grading programme combines striking, grappling, physical fitness and practical application in one structured journey. Students progress from White Level to Black Level, with assessment outcomes recorded as A, B, C or Fail.

Developed for MMA Colombo Combat Club students, the system supports both personal progress in martial arts and the ambition to train as a coach and build a coaching career.

About our grading programme

What is the MMA Colombo grading system?

MMA Colombo Combat Club offers a structured, nine-level grading system exclusively for students training at the club. The levels are White, Yellow, Orange, Red, Green, Blue, Purple, Brown and Black. Training covers four categories: Striking, Grappling, Fitness, and Pad Training & Sparring. Assessment outcomes are A, B, C or Fail. The system supports student development and a pathway towards a coaching career.

Which martial arts are included in the grading system?

Striking draws on Muay Thai, Kickboxing, Sanda, Boxing and Combat Sambo. Grappling draws on Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ), Judo, Combat Sambo and Wrestling. Fitness, pad training and sparring complete the four training categories.

Who can take part in MMA Colombo gradings?

The grading programme is exclusively for students training at MMA Colombo Combat Club. Current students can speak to their coach about preparation and grading arrangements. Prospective students can contact the club about joining.

Does the grading system support a coaching career?

Yes. Alongside developing martial arts skills, MMA Colombo's grading system is designed to help committed students work towards becoming coaches and developing a coaching career. Speak to the club's coaches about the training and requirements for this pathway.
